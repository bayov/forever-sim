Forever beta test log, level 30 Orc shaman
==========================================

Every result from the beta tests for the shaman audit (shaman_audit.md), with the raw hits
from the combat log screenshots.

How to read the hits:
- Each number is the whole hit: the shown amount plus the overkill.
- Crits are listed apart, at their full value. Melee crits are 2x a normal hit and spell
  crits 1.5x.
- Glancing blows are left out.
- "Normal" means not a crit and not glancing.


Weapons used
------------
- Barbaric Battle Axe of Healing: two-hand axe, 25 - 38, 3.6 speed, +5 Int, +6 spell damage,
  +19 healing
- Twin-bladed Axe of the Owl: two-hand axe, 23 - 35, 2.7 speed, +3 Int, +3 Spi
- Bloody Brass Knuckles: one-hand fist, 24 - 46, 1.6 speed, +12 AP (+15 more vs humanoids)

"ap X Y Z" lines are UnitAttackPower: base, plus, minus.

Flametongue Weapon formula that fits every hit so far
-----------------------------------------------------
hit = (tooltip max x weapon speed / 4 - cut + 10% of spell power)
      x (1 + 5% per Elemental Weapons point)

- Tooltip max is the second number of the tooltip with 0 spell power.
- Cut at level 30: rank 1 0, rank 2 about 2, rank 3 about 5. Ranks 4 to 6 are unknown.
- The 10% of spell power is the sim's value. 6 spell power is too little to confirm it.
