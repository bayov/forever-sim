## Effects modelled

All values from client 1.60.1.71037, in `sim/common/item_effects.go`. Tests are in
`sim/warrior/dps_warrior/items_phase0_test.go`.

- **Headmaster's Charge (13937):** Use gives +20 Intellect for 15 min on a 10 min cooldown (spell 18264). The sim gives it to the wearer only. Test `TestHeadmastersChargeUse`. EFFECTS_ONLY on frost mage: 1.8 DPS.
- **Axe of the Deep Woods (811):** Wrath (18104) deals 105 to 147 Nature at 1.7 PPM, under Forever only. The PPM is from wowsims/forever's client store. Test `TestAxeOfTheDeepWoodsProc`. EFFECTS_ONLY on dual-wield fury: 12.1 DPS.
- **Flame Wrath (11809), a fix:** its fire shield hit back the wearer's own damage and set itself off without end. That crashed the arms and fury two-hand search with a stack overflow. Test `TestFlameWrathShieldIgnoresSelfDamage`.

- **An empty weapon slot no longer swings (a fix):** with no ranged weapon, a hunter's Auto Shot came due again in the same instant without end. The ranking sims the set with the ranged slot empty, so every hunter ranged ranking hung. A wait on the next Auto Shot also overflowed. Both are fixed in `sim/core/attack.go` and `apl_actions_timing.go`. Test `TestNoRangedWeaponFinishes`.

## Effects left unmodelled

There's a comment for each in `item_effects.go`:

- **Book of the Dead (13353):** summons a Skeleton (17490) for 60s. The client has no stats for it. Its Forever equip aura 1298517 is a dummy with no effect the client shows. It ranks 2nd in the demonology off hand without the summon.
- **Demonfork (12621):** drains 50 health over 5s at an unknown rate.
- **No damage to a boss:** Julie's Dagger heals, and Hammer of the Titans and The Unstoppable Force only stun.

## Content and enchants

- **phase0_content.json:** 132 new entries, all sourced from AtlasLoot, Wowhead and the db. Of these:
  - 19 are doubtful, because their source isn't confirmed open (Hyjal, Blackmaw Hold, Alterac Valley, summoned bosses) or no source is known.
  - 5 are infeasible: Teebu's Blazing Longsword at about 3500g, plus Arcanite Champion, Annihilator, Heartseeker and Frostguard.
  - 4 are out: Sageblade, whose plans drop from the world dragons, and three Alliance-only reputation items.
- **phase0_enchants.json:**
  - Lesser Intellect (2H) is open.
  - Lesser Spirit (2H) is doubtful. Its formula is a 0.3% world drop.
  - Winter's Might is doubtful. Its formula has no known source.
  - Off-Hand Wisdom is open, for 240 of currency 3402.
- **Hunter additions:**
  - The four engineering scopes are open.
  - Rhok'delar and Lok'delar are out, since they need Molten Core items.
  - Ankle Slicers and Hyper Deluxe Sniper Rifle Mk XVII are doubtful, with no known source.
  - Demonbane Knife is doubtful, as a Horde quest in Hyjal.
- **Final check:** with the current files, no spec's rank list (all 23, hunters included) has an unlisted item or enchant in these slots.
