# Phase 0 gear step 2: cloth and leather, 2026-11-22

Brief: `briefs/phase0_gear_step2_1122.md`. Slots: head, shoulders, chest, wrists, hands, waist, legs and feet in
cloth and leather, for every DPS spec in `tools/rotopt/phase0_specs.json`.

## How the candidates were found

Each spec ran `RANK_ONLY=1 SLOTS=head,shoulder,chest,wrist,hands,waist,legs,feet` from a stub `FROM` with no picks
(`{"durations":[180],"use_dur":90,"slots":{},"enchants":{}}`), at low iterations (`-iters 300
-gear-rank-confirm-iters 1000`, the last specs at 200 and 600). A spec took 20 to 30 min on 4 cores. The top 10 cloth
or leather items per slot and spec count as candidates, and the top 5 enchants.

Specs ranked: affliction, balance, demonology, destruction, ele, enh, feral, fury, fury_dw, hunter_bm, mage_arcane, mage_fire, mage_frost, mage_frostfire, ret, rogue_assassination, rogue_combat_swords, rogue_subtlety, shadow, smite. hunter_mm and hunter_sv share hunter_bm's stats and gear, and arms shares fury's, so they
weren't ranked apart. A hunter with no gear does 0 DPS and the ranking writes NaN, so hunter_bm started from the report's synthetic gear in every slot but the 8 armor slots.

## Effects

Only 5 cloth or leather items in the pools had an unmodelled "Use:" or "Chance on hit:" effect. Three are modelled in
`sim/common/forever_armor_uses.go`, with the Forever values from client 1.60.1.71037 (ItemXItemEffect, ItemEffect,
SpellEffect, SpellMisc), and a test each in `forever_armor_uses_test.go`:

- **8348 Helm of Fire** (leather head): Fireball for 286 to 376 Fire, then 33 every 2s for 8s (Classic 10). 5 min
  cooldown (Classic 15 min) and 10s on the burst item category the Forever trinkets share. `TestForeverHelmOfFire`
  checks the cooldowns, the ticks and the 33. `EFFECTS_ONLY` on feral: +6.6 DPS.
- **11808 Circle of Flame** (cloth head): a 10s channel giving 59 to 99 mana a second (Classic 56 to 94), 5 min
  cooldown. Pressed when 1000 or more mana short. The health it costs isn't modelled. `TestForeverCircleOfFlame`
  checks the channel, the cooldown and 2000 ticks' range and mean. `EFFECTS_ONLY` on fire mage at 180s: -60 DPS, the
  channel costs more casting than the mana gives back in a short fight.
- **14152 Robe of the Archmage** (cloth chest): 375 to 625 mana, off the GCD, 5 min cooldown of its own. Pressed when
  625 short. `TestForeverRobeOfTheArchmage` checks no GCD, 2000 casts' range and mean, and the cooldown.
  `EFFECTS_ONLY` on fire mage: +1.7 DPS.

Left out, with a comment in the file:

- 12185 Bloodsail Admiral's Hat: the use summons a parrot (17567) with no combat use.
- 14153 Robe of the Void: Heal Pet (18386) for 300 to 900 on the warlock's demon. The sim's demons aren't attacked.
