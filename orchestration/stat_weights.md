# Stat weights for every Level 60 preset

Measured on the merged build of all nine class branches (heads as of 2026-10-13 05:30 UTC). Each stat was added as a bonus stat on top of the preset (same path as gear, so Forever hit/crit unification and stat multipliers apply) and simmed at 180 s, 40,000 iterations, the baseline's seed. "Worth" is in points of the preset's main stat (AP, RAP or SP). Hit and crit are per 1% and per rating point (10 hit rating, 14 crit rating = 1%). "Hit -1%" is what losing 1% costs (a preset at the cap gains nothing from more). One standard error is about 0.01-0.02 per point for main stats and 0.3-0.5 DPS per 1%.

Each build's hit now sits at its own step's value, so the Hit columns moved most (a build that went down a step to the cap now loses a lot from the next 1% down, as Paladin and Enhancement do; the rogues, who went up a step, value hit and crit about alike). The other rows are unchanged from before.
The four hunter rows were measured again on 2026-10-25 with `tools/dps_report/weights.py` (180 s, seed 1, paired, 5,000 to 40,000 iterations) on that day's presets, after the hunter's crit per Agility became Classic's at 60 (Forever's PlayerExpectedStat; it was 22% more).
