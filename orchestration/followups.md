# Follow-ups after the nine class PRs

Items found late that don't affect any shipped Level 60 preset, left out of the class PRs to avoid another merge round across all nine branches.

- **Creature-type damage auras also multiply crit damage** (found by the rogue's talent verification, 2026-10-12). The client's creature-type damage auras (aura 168) raise damage only, but the shared code multiplies both DamageDealtMultiplier and CritMultiplier, so a crit comes out about 2.08x instead of 2x:
  - sim/core/racials.go (Troll Beast Slaying, 20557);
  - sim/core/racials_forever.go mobTypeDamageAura (Skyborne Elemental Insight);
  - sim/common/item_sets/item_sets_pve.go (the anti-Undead set bonuses).
  None fires against the Humanoid Level 60 boss, so no preset moves. The rogue fixed its own Murder talent (same pattern) in its PR. Hunter Humanoid/Monster Slaying (19151, 24293) do carry a second crit-damage aura, so the hunter code is correct.

- **Rage needs a beta test** (user, 2026-10-13: "rage needs to be well-tested eventually, for now we're making assumptions"). Standards 21 and `warr_rage_k` model Forever's Rage from beta testers' level 30 numbers and Blizzard's notes. None of it has been measured at 60. Until it is, every item below stays an assumption in spec_assumptions.md, and the dashboard shows its range. The test plan, for the highest level the beta allows, logging every Rage gain against the swing that caused it:
  1. **Main-hand white hits, normalised k.** Use a slow and a fast one-hander, and a slow and a fast two-hander. Swing a training dummy (or a mob at +3 levels) with no talents that add Rage. Check that Rage per hit = k × swing speed, independent of damage, AP and gear. The level 30 numbers are 3.46 (1H) and 4.5 (2H). Repeat at two levels to see whether k scales with level.
  2. **Off-hand factor.** Settled at ×0.5 before the talent by a beta combat log with advanced logging (Arlewow's gist https://gist.github.com/Arlewow/a622100ad149f4fc31ddf95f9b3aab2c, linked from magey/forever-warrior issue 3, 20 September, level 13 warrior with a 1.8 sec main hand and 1.6 sec off hand dagger) shows 6.2 to 6.3 Rage a main hand swing (3.46 × 1.8) and 2.7 to 2.8 an off hand swing (3.46 × 1.6 × 0.5), glancing blows included, on about 50 and 60 clean swings (assumptions audit, 2026-10-16). Still open: whether Dual Wield Specialization's off-hand Rage (10% a point since 1 October) applies on top as the sim assumes; the same weapons in the off hand with and without the talent show it.
  3. **Crits.** Settled by Blizzard's 1 October notes (+100%, "was 75%"; assumptions audit 2026-10-16). Since 2026-10-23 the bonus is 100% of the one-hand-rate Rage, before the two-hand factor (wowsims/forever 5ac4ffbcb6, Rule of Rage row 1322574). A log can still confirm it: compare the Rage of crit and non-crit white hits from the same weapon, a two-hander included.
  4. **Glancing blows, dodges and parries.** Check whether a glancing blow gives full k × speed. Dodges and parries: since 2026-10-23 the sim gives nothing for them, as wowsims/forever's level 8 and 9 logs show (f9f9f21883).
  5. **Non-white damage.** Check that Heroic Strike, Cleave, Execute, Deep Wounds and other ability damage give no Rage from damage dealt.
  6. **Rage from damage taken.** Blizzard: the original formula, "as if you always had 50% damage reduction from Armor", absorbs ignored. The 8 October notes add that it no longer uses the player's health, and that crits and crushing blows count. Since 2026-10-23 the sim uses wowsims/forever's fit on post-8 October melee logs (2.5 × (1 − A) × damage / the conversion at the attacker's level, A by the attacker's level; ee2d69b16a, 73b317c7cf, de550b7df9). Still to measure: spell hits taken (their logs show 1 to 2 times the melee rule), attackers above 60, and Berserker Rage's doubling. It matters to tanks and, through the raid damage hits (standards 24), to the DPS presets.
  7. **Bear Form.** Check whether the same rules apply to a druid in Bear Form. The druid has no DPS preset there.
  Owner: the warrior worker turns the results into sim constants and tests, and moves each measured item out of spec_assumptions.md.

- **Shadow Oil's proc damage needs a beta log** (user, 2026-10-13). Forever's tooltip says 63 to 71, the server formula Era's 52 to 61; the sim uses the tooltip and the dashboard shows Era's as `oil_proc_damage` Low. One Shadow Oil hit above 61 at level 60 settles `oil_proc_damage`.

- **Shadow Oil's 10 sec cooldown has no source** (shaman audit 9.9, the user's OK 2026-10-15). `sim/core/consumes.go` applyWeaponImbueConsumes gives Shadow Oil a 10 sec cooldown shared by both hands. It came from the upstream SoD sim (2024-02, "add Frost/Shadow Oils") with no reason given. Client 70338's enchant 25 holds only the 15% chance, and Frost Oil in the sim has no cooldown. Without it the Level 60 Enhancement request goes from 813.9 to 832.5 at 180 sec (Shadow Oil 22.1 to 41.7 DPS), and the Retribution builds move too. The sim keeps it until the beta settles it (notes.md Need to Verify): with no cooldown some procs come seconds apart, with it none are under 10 sec apart.

- **Class-cast debuffs at low level** (shaman audit 10.4, 2026-10-15). The raid debuffs now take the rank for the raid's level under Forever, but a class's own cast doesn't. `core.SunderArmorAura` is fixed at 450 a stack, `FaerieFireAura` and Forever's `CurseOfRecklessnessAura` at 505, and `ExposeArmorAura` at 2250, so a warrior, druid, warlock or rogue below level 54 to 58 gets too much. The warrior's own Demoralizing Shout (`sim/warrior/demoralizing_shout.go`) is Classic's with Improved 5, where Forever's rank 5 takes 204 at 60 for 45 sec with no Improved talent (`foreverDemoralizingShout` in `debuffs.go`). The paladin's own Judgement of the Crusader (`sim/paladin/sotc.go`) gives 1.15 x Classic, so 34.5 / 57.5 / 126.5 where the client has 35 / 58 / 127. None of it moves a Level 60 DPS preset.
