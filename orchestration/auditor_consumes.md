# Auditor: consumables, buffs and debuffs

Audit each Level 60 preset's consumables, raid buffs, party buffs, individual buffs (blessings) and debuffs (presets.ts DefaultConsumes, DefaultRaidBuffs, DefaultIndividualBuffs, DefaultDebuffs, the preset builds and encounters, and the matching tools/rotopt/mksettings/spec_<name>.go). Don't run optimisers; reason it out from the code (sim/core/consumes.go, buffs.go, debuffs.go and the class code), the Forever client data and the spec's needs. Check: every consumable is one that exists and is obtainable in Forever Phase 1 and works as the sim models it under Forever; nothing is missing that the spec would clearly use (flask, elixirs of the right kind, food, weapon oil or stone or imbue, potions, runes, explosives with the profession, Forever's own new consumables); nothing conflicts or double counts (elixir categories, food buffs, imbue stacking under Forever); the raid buffs and blessings fit the spec's group (Wisdom vs Might, Mana Spring vs Windfury / Strength of Earth / Grace of Air, Trueshot, Moonkin, Leader of the Pack, Bloodlust-type Forever buffs); debuffs fit the spec (Sunder / Expose / Faerie Fire / Curse of Recklessness for physical, Curse of the Elements for magic and not Curse of Shadow, no Improved Scorch / Winter's Chill for non-mages, the spec's own debuffs not double counted with the raid's); world buffs are off; professions are consistent with the consumables and gear; and the classes use the same baseline raid so their numbers compare. Where something is wrong or missing, estimate its DPS effect from the code and spell data and send the worker the finding.
