# Level 60 gear stat-mix targets (standards 5a)

BUNDLED ROUND, 2026-10-13 (supersedes the 07:05 table, which is in git history at e3f2ae044). Generated by orchestration/tools/gear_targets_v3.py (output gear_targets_v3.json).

How the targets are derived:
- Source: wowsims/sod level 60 BiS sets (the files are listed in the script). Trinkets are excluded, AP and RAP count once, and SoD's weapon feral AP is excluded.
- Pricing: the standards 1b weights. Spell power is 0.86 and school-only power 0.70, folded into SP. AP is 0.5, RAP 0.4, hit and crit rating 1.0, MP5 2.4.
- Stamina: each spec's own SoD average share (user ruling: per-spec Stamina).
- Spirit: 10% for mana users (standards 5). That includes hunters (user ruling) and Ret / Enhancement.
- Other stats: SoD average proportions, re-normalised to the rest.
- Ret and Enhancement (standards 5b): Intellect and spell power keep the 5b amounts (Ret 11.1 / 20.2, Enh 11.7 / 22.1 at spell power 0.55), re-priced at 0.86. The physical stats follow SoD.
- Smite: shadow priest proportions without hit.
- Rogue swords: Agility is clipped at the 45% guardrail (not binding at the new weights).

Shares are linear (L): budget weight x amount / the set's weighted total, enchants and trinkets excluded, AP and RAP counted once.

Rules for the round:
- Budget per piece, weapons and trinkets: standards 1b (per archetype, with the 1.7-norm shape check).
- Hit: item 4 decides. Hit goes to the spec's cap at the best mix, counting talents and the 2-piece bonus; the table's hit column is only SoD's own share. Hit is set first, and the other non-Stamina, non-Spirit stats keep the table's proportions in the room left.
- Tolerance: every non-Stamina stat within +/- 3 points (L) of its HIT-ADJUSTED target; Stamina within +/- 1.5. The hit-adjusted target takes the set's actual capped hit share, then re-scales the other non-Stamina, non-Spirit targets in the table's proportions to fill the room left. Example: Shadow at 10.8% hit gives SP 37.0, Int 15.2, Crit 10.4. The table's own figures apply only where hit matches SoD's share.
- Guardrail: no one stat over 55% of the non-Stamina share. The last column shows the top stat's share.
- Mage: one shared set for Frostfire, Frost and Fire; Arcane keeps its own.
- Warlock: one set for Destruction, Affliction and Fire; Demonology keeps its own.

## Healers (2026-11-03)

The user's ruling, 2026-11-03: "yes, healer synthetic gear", from Wowhead's lists, since wowsims/sod has no
level 60 BiS sets for any healer. The healing sim's healers (docs/healing_sim.md) wear sets built on this row.

- **Source:** two Wowhead lists, averaged as the SoD lists are: the WoW Forever Restoration shaman Phase 1 BiS
  guide (www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/shaman/restoration/bis-gear, "a rough estimate based on the
  stat priority of Spell Healing, Critical Strike, Haste, Spirit and Mp5", with Forever's own items and Era
  ones) and the Classic Restoration shaman pre-raid BiS guide
  (www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/shaman-healing-pre-raid-best-in-slot-bis-gear-wow-classic). Each slot's
  first BiS pick, the two-hander path, two rings, no trinkets or relic. Where the Forever guide's pick has no
  such item in our database (its BiS chest, Masterwork Breastplate "of Healing", has no Healing roll), the
  slot's next pick. "Of Healing" world drops at their Healing roll.
- **Pricing:** the standards 1b weights, with healing on top of spell power at 0.45 (a separate column here).
  The Forever list prices at Sta 10.8, Str 1.9, Int 14.3, Spi 7.3, SP 37.9, healing 9.3, hit 3.2, crit 11.1,
  MP5 4.2; the Classic one at Sta 10.8, Int 20.1, Spi 10.4, SP 5.7, healing 43.8, crit 2.7, MP5 6.4. Forever's
  items carry their healing as spell power, Era's as healing alone.
- **Target:** Stamina the lists' own share, Spirit 10% (standards 5), no hit (heals don't miss), no Strength
  (one Tier 0.5 chest's), the rest in the lists' average proportions. Tolerances as for the other rows.
  Scripts: the session scratchpad's heal_mix.py and synth_heal.py; the numbers are their output.

## Tanks (2026-11-03)

The tank workers' brief (orchestration/brief_tanks.md, the user: "implement all tank specs"): every tank wears a
synthetic set by standards 36. Scripts: orchestration/tools/tank_prices.py (the tank stat prices) and
gear_targets_tanks.py (these rows and the alternatives' effect; `python3 gear_targets_tanks.py <wowsims/sod>/ui`).

- **Source:** wowsims/sod's level 60 tank gear sets (commit 0e3f6eff5f, 2026-07-24), averaged as the DPS rows:
  - Protection warrior: `tank_warrior` phase_4_tanky and phase_4_damage;
  - Protection paladin: `protection_paladin` p4prot and p5prot;
  - Feral tank: `feral_tank_druid` phase_6, phase_7_prebis and phase_7 (it has no phase 4-5 bear set; the
    catweave set left out);
  - Warden shaman: `warden_shaman` phase_4_enh_tank. Our Warden is a melee shield tank (Stormstrike), so SoD's
    Elemental tank list (a spell power tank) is left out; its row is at the end of the script's output (SP 27.0,
    Int 16.6, Def 5.1).
  Wowhead wasn't needed: wowsims/sod has a list for every tank.
- **Pricing:** standards 1b with standards 36's tank prices (Defense 1.5, dodge 12, parry 20 and block 5 a 1%,
  block value 0.4, resistance 1.0). Trinkets left out, AP and RAP once, SoD's weapon feral AP left out, as the DPS
  rows. SoD's expertise and haste (bear phase 6-7 gear) aren't priced in 1b and drop out. Armor is free; SoD's
  db doesn't tell bonus armor from base armor, so none is counted.
- **Target:** Stamina the spec's own SoD share (tanks carry 24-33%, against 14-21% for DPS sets); Spirit 10% for
  the paladin and the shaman (standards 5); no resistances (standards 36: SoD's warrior, paladin and shaman lists
  put 11% of their budget into them, mostly fire for Molten Core); the rest in SoD's proportions.
- **Rules for the sets:** standards 36 (budget by the class's archetype, the 0.72 x L(71) shield, the tank
  trinket), 5a's tolerances (+/- 3 points a stat, Stamina +/- 1.5, the 55% guardrail) and 4 for hit, read as
  the tank objective rather than DPS.
- **SoD's tank lists carry no dodge or parry** (their Defense took that room), so the rows have none. Era's tank
  epics put 6.6% of their budget into dodge and 3.2% into parry; a spec whose objective wants them can move
  points there inside the tolerance.
- **The paladin's lists carry no Intellect** (and almost no spell power): Lawbringer and Judgement are Strength,
  Stamina and Defense. A spec that runs short of mana can say so in its report.
