# Level 60 gear stat-mix targets (standards 5a)

From the fairness auditor's report section 7b (2026-10-13 06:44 UTC), derived from wowsims/sod level 60 BiS sets (two late-phase sets per spec, p6 or p5, Naxx sets excluded) and orchestration/sod_stat_mix.md. Target = SoD proportions re-normalised to Stamina 18%, Spirit 10% for mana users (item 5), hit held at the current set's share (item 4; re-check the cap after the rebudget). Linear (L) shares: budget weight x amount / the set's weighted total, enchants excluded, AP and RAP counted once.

Orchestrator rulings (06:50 UTC):
- Tolerance: every non-Stamina stat within +/- 3 points (L) of its target; Stamina 18 +/- 1.5.
- Smite: target A (SoD shadow priest proportions, shadow-only SP counted as generic SP, no hit).
- Mage: one shared set for Frostfire, Frost and Fire (the averaged SoD fire and frost proportions below, school SP taken as generic SP since standards 5a allows school-only SP only where real gear for the spec carries it); Arcane keeps its own set with its own target.
- Ret paladin, Enhancement shaman and hunters: on hold for two user decisions (whether Ret and Enh keep today's spell power share; whether hunters keep 10% Spirit).
