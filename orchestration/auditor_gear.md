# Auditor: Phase 0 gear

The DPS presets wear their spec's Phase 0 list since 2026-11-24 (orchestration/standards.md 43): real items obtainable before Forever's first raids, found by `tools/rotopt/bis_phase0.sh` and tagged by hand in `tools/rotopt/phase0_content.json` and `phase0_enchants.json`. Audit every list (`ui/<dir>/bis/level60_phase0*.bis.json`) and the gear set the report wears (`ui/<dir>/gear_sets/level60_phase0*.gear.json`). For each pick: is it in the pool (dungeons, quests, crafting, world drops, the auction house, reputation up to Revered, PvP reputations), never raid or world boss loot or a raid-material craft, never an Exalted reward or an item over the gold line (those are ranked but not picked), and never the other faction's reward for the build's race; does its tag and note say what is unsure about it (standards 42); is a use, proc or hidden stat the sim doesn't model flagged. For each list: are the enchants open ones, is the weapon setup the spec's, do the set bonuses it reaches work in the sim and was a set whose bonus needs several pieces at once tried as a whole, is the build's hit sensible against its cap with the hit talents re-checked, does an open item sit above a pick (the search stopped short), and does the report's request wear the list's set and sim to its dps. Race overrides of the other faction must swap the faction slots for their own faction's best. The synthetic budget checks (standards 1b) now belong to the tank and healer gear auditors.
