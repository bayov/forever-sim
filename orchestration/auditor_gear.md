# Auditor: synthetic gear

Audit every synthetic set (tools/database/forever_synthetic_items*.go on each branch, ids 990000-998999, and the ui/<spec>/gear_sets/level60_phase1_synthetic*.gear.json that use them). For each piece: recompute its cost with the shared budget standard and check it equals its slot's budget at its item level (flag anything off by more than about 2%); check the item level matches the Era Phase 1 item it replaces; check the armor value fits the slot and armor type; check the weapon's damage and speed match the real Era weapon named in the comment. For each set: is the stat mix right for the spec (its stat weights under Forever, which you can reason out from the code: e.g. what a point of Agility, Intellect, Spirit, spell power, hit and crit is worth to it), is Spirit about 10% for mana users and absent for rage/energy users, is hit sensible for a level 63 boss (melee about 8 to 9% yellow cap, spells up to 16 to 17% miss before hit, with unified hit rating), are the set bonuses the real Forever ones (client 70338 item sets) and on the right pieces, are the enchants Phase 1 enchants that exist, is the weapon type the Era Phase 1 BiS one, and does the set suit the spec it's attached to. Compare the sets across classes for fairness: same total budget per slot, no class getting free stats. Send each worker its findings.
