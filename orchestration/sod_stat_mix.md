# Our Level 60 gear sets against Season of Discovery level 60 BiS

Corrected 2026-10-13 07:10 UTC after the fairness auditor's check: trinkets excluded (our sets carry none, item 8a), AP and RAP on one item counted once, SoD weapon feral attack power excluded (a SoD-only mechanic), school-only spell power weighted 0.45, haste shown but left out of the total.

Linear share of each stat in a set (standards 5a metric): budget weight x amount / the set's total, enchants excluded. Weights: Str / Agi / Sta / Int / Spi 1.0, spell power 0.55 (generic SP and spell damage together), school-only SP 0.45, AP 0.5, RAP 0.4 (one AP column), hit 10 and crit 14 points per 1% (an item's "+1% hit" counts once even if it sets both the melee and the spell stat), MP5 2.5.

SoD sets: wowsims/sod master, ui/<spec>/gear_sets, level 60 phases (4 and later), items from its assets/database/db.json. Set bonuses, procs, use effects and runes are not counted, and SoD gives hit from runes, talents and set bonuses that our standard puts on gear (item 4), so SoD hit shares read low and our hit shares high by design. Smite has no SoD equivalent; it is compared with SoD shadow priest sets.
