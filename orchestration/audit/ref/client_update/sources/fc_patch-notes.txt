##  October 15, 2026   Build 1.60.1.70425  Current build 
From  Blizzard’s notes :
## General  
- A new tutorial for new players. 
- Wands now need you to stand still for 0.5 sec before they shoot, as in early World of Warcraft. 
- Horde players can no longer gain Kirin Tor reputation, monsters regenerate health out of combat again, drink tooltips show the right total mana, and the fog is no longer too bright at low shadow settings.    
## Items  
- Mystic Mushroom and the Insight enchant now raise Spirit as they should. Scrolls now trigger the global cooldown. 
- Many crafted items sell to vendors for less. 
- Draught of Predatory Senses becomes a potion, shares the combat potion cooldown, and lasts less.    
## Legacy  
- Bartering no longer discounts training and repairs. Master Chef and Luremaster move one column left on the tree (they already needed only 5 points). The Quick and the Dead affects more Priest spells.    
## PvP  
- Battleground Marks of Honor stack to 30 (was 20).    
## Quests  
- Dalaran: the Horde is attacked on sight in the city, whatever the quest. The City of Dalaran dungeon stays open to them, but the Dalaran Sewer Key questline no longer opens the city in the open world. 
- More Bloodtooth Guards for “Raene’s Cleansing” (Ashenvale). 
- The furbolg camps of “How Big a Threat?” give credit (Darkshore). 
- “For Love Eternal” can always be turned in. 
- Cenarion Moondust is no longer used up in “Body and Heart”. 
- The Novice Warriors of “Sparring Match” no longer flag you for PvP (Mulgore). 
- “The Attack!” is no longer an escort and can be shared (Stormwind). 
- Lord Melenas no longer evades in “Twisted Hatred” (Teldrassil). 
- Faster respawns in the Wetlands. 
- Halaan’s Gift works again for “The Anchors of Zephras”.    
## Interface  
- Hunters can turn on an Auto Attack/Auto Shot setting that switches between the two on its own, gamepad included. 
- Bloom Intensity becomes a slider, in the Advanced graphics options. 
- New unit frames and minimap header. 
- The Auction House gets a column for the level needed to equip, and shows the stats of random-suffix items (“of the Monkey”). 
- Level-up messages show stat gains and talent points again. 
- /who finds subzones. 
- Spell ranks count as one entry in the Cooldown Manager. 
- Enemy and NPC nameplates far above or below you show at a shorter distance (your target’s is unchanged). 
- More Scrolling Combat Text options. 
- Gamepad: a swing timer on the center reticle, and trade, guild, chat channels and transmog windows usable with a gamepad.   
Found in the game:
- Nine white items become green, with a stat: Ice-covered Bracers, Royal Frostmane Girdle, Wendigo Fur Cloak, Wispy Cloak, Ghostly Bracers, Ratty Old Belt, Lithe Boots, Fingerless Gloves and Staff of the Mountain. 
- The Tinkerbox, its three modules and their seven engineering schematics are gone from the client, as are the Disorienting Smog Potion and its recipe. 
- Sixteen centaur items (Kolkar and Magram) now need level 35, and twelve of them drop to item level 38 or 42 (was 43).  
##  Warrior  
-   Buffs 
    Battle Shout :  Now covers the whole raid.  
-   Buffs 
    Slam :  No longer suffers spell pushback.  
-   Buffs 
    Overpower :  Can trigger against much lower level enemies again.  
-   Buffs 
    Lingering Rage :  No longer stops your health regeneration after combat.  
-   Buffs 
    Improved Berserker Rage :  Removes movement-impairing effects again.  
-   Nerfs 
    Weaponmaster :  Its Sword Specialization can no longer trigger from Shield Bash and Shield Slam.     
##  Paladin  
-   Buffs 
    Righteous Fury :  Holy damage threat raised to 90% (was 60%).  
-   Nerfs 
    Seal of Righteousness :  Got almost twice the spell damage it should: fixed. Judgement of Righteousness and Judgement of Fury get the instant-spell coefficients.  
-   Nerfs 
    Consecration :  Gets 4.2% of spell power per tick (was 9.5%).  
-   Nerfs 
    Infusion of Light :  No longer gives Holy Shock 50% more damage against enemies. Light’s Vigil works with focus macros.  
-   Nerfs 
    Shield Specialization :  Restores 4% mana (was 6%), still every 3 sec at most.  
-   Nerfs 
    Champion of the Light :  Gives spell damage equal to 10/20/30% of your Intellect (was 20/40/60%).  
-   Changes 
    Salvation :  Replaces Blessing of Salvation, and Mass Salvation replaces Greater Blessing of Salvation: cast the same way, but they no longer take a Blessing slot. You also learn Revoke, which removes them from a party or raid member.  
-   Changes 
    Seal of Fury :  Is no longer overwritten by Power Word: Shield. Its rank 3 is trained at level 26 (was 25).  
-   Changes 
    Reckoning :  Stores up to 4 charges again, as in Classic Era: each trigger adds one, and your next swing spends them all as extra attacks.  
-   Changes 
    Sanctified Judgement :  Its mana return no longer generates threat.  
-   Changes 
    Sense Undead :  Is no longer in the spellbook. Not in Blizzard’s notes, found in the game.      
##  Hunter  
-   Buffs 
    Immolation Trap :  Fire traps scale with attack power, and you can have one fire trap and one frost trap out at once (was one trap in all).  
-   Buffs 
    Pets :  Benefit from Expertise, and take your higher critical chance, melee or ranged (was always melee).  
-   Buffs 
    Summon Hawk :  Spawns the hawk at your target and keeps it there.  
-   Buffs 
    Improved Mend Pet :  Lowers Mend Pet’s mana cost instead of raising it.  
-   Buffs 
    Trueshot Aura :  Now covers the whole raid. Sniper Shot benefits from the Marksmanship talents it missed.  
-   Buffs 
    Strider Kick :  Can be used while disarmed or with no main-hand weapon, and Resourcefulness lowers its cost.  
-   Buffs 
    Multi-Shot :  Is now instant (was a 0.5 sec cast). Not in Blizzard’s notes, found in the game.   
-   Nerfs 
    Auto Shot :  Needs you to stand still for 0.5 sec before it shoots, as in early World of Warcraft. You still fire it while casting Aimed Shot or Sniper Shot.  
-   Nerfs 
    Dismember :  Crocolisk ability: healing reduction 25% (was 50%), 6 sec (was 10), 15 sec cooldown (was 6).  
-   Nerfs 
    Sonic Blast :  Bat ability: cast slow 30% (was 60%), 20 sec (was 30), 60 sec cooldown (was 30).  
-   Nerfs 
    Mine! :  Bird of Prey ability: 3 sec (was 4), 90 sec cooldown (was 60).  
-   Changes 
    Resourcefulness :  Triggers on critical strikes again, but only from melee and ranged abilities, not traps.  
-   Changes 
    Bows :  Show the arrow while drawn.  
-   Changes 
    Predator's Edge :  Its tooltip now says it only raises your abilities’ critical damage.     
##  Rogue  
-   Nerfs 
    Poisons :  No longer trigger against targets attacking you, and Sap no longer triggers weapon procs.  
-   Nerfs 
    Energy :  Swapping specializations no longer keeps it full.  
-   Nerfs 
    Cold Blood :  Is spent when Mutilate crits.     
##  Priest  
-   Buffs 
    Devouring Plague :  Cooldown drops to 24 sec (was 1 min).  
-   Buffs 
    Power in Light :  Also raises Smite and Penance on a target with Holy Fire or Shadow Word: Pain.  
-   Buffs 
    Silent Resolve :  Its threat reduction applies to all spells.  
-   Buffs 
    Twin Disciplines :  Also works with Shadow Word: Death, Mind Flay, Starshards and Penance.  
-   Buffs 
    Shadow Word: Death :  Benefits from Shadowform’s critical damage bonus.  
-   Buffs 
    Vampiric Embrace :  Can’t be resisted, and its cooldown drops to 30 sec (was 1 min).  
-   Buffs 
    Dark Inspiration :  New Shadow talent on row 4: Mind Blast and a full Mind Flay raise your party’s Spirit by 3/6/9/12/15% for 10 sec.  
-   Buffs 
    Devouring Contagion :  Spreads to 2 targets, and the copied Devouring Plague no longer costs mana or puts yours on cooldown.  
-   Nerfs 
    Shadow Word: Death :  Cooldown rises to 18 sec (was 12).  
-   Changes 
    Renew :  A high rank no longer stops other priests’ Renew.  
-   Changes 
    Holy Fire :  Rank 5 no longer ticks harder than rank 6.  
-   Changes 
    Mind Vision :  No longer makes the target stand up and stop drinking.  
-   Changes 
    Inner Focus :  Is spent on one spell only, even when a spell is queued during Penance. Divine Aegis’s tooltip says it needs a non-periodic critical heal and gives its shield’s maximum.  
-   Changes 
    Improved Fade :  Removed, as is Shadow Affinity (Blizzard’s notes call it Shadow Infinity). Improved Mind Blast moves to row 2 (was 3).  
-   Changes 
    Improved Mind Flay :  Each Mind Flay tick deals 10/20% more than the one before.  
-   Changes 
    Mental Strength  moved :  Moves to row 2 (was 4) and raises Intellect by 2/4/6/8/10%. Improved Power Word: Shield moves to row 3 (was 2).     
##  Shaman  
-   Buffs 
    Grace of Air Totem :  Now covers the whole raid, as do Strength of Earth, Windfury and Flametongue Totems. It no longer excludes Windfury and Flametongue, which still exclude each other, and those two only affect players.  
-   Buffs 
    Chain Lightning :  Costs about 35% less mana at every rank.  
-   Buffs 
    Elemental Mastery :  Returns as the 31-point gold medal talent: Lightning Bolt and Lava Burst cast 0.5 sec faster and Chain Lightning has no cooldown for 15 sec, 3 min cooldown. Needs Lava Burst.  
-   Buffs 
    Elemental Reach :  Also lowers Totemic Projection’s cooldown by 10/20 sec, and Call of Flame extends Flame Shock by 3/6/9 sec.  
-   Buffs 
    Lightning Overload :  No longer spends Clearcasting, and Elemental Focus can no longer be spent on the Fire Nova that triggered it.  
-   Buffs 
    Mana Tide Totem :  Gets a new rank at level 38 (ranks at 25, 38, 48 and 58).  
-   Buffs 
    Restorative Totems :  Raises all of Healing Stream Totem’s healing (was its base value).  
-   Buffs 
    Water Shield :  Can trigger from any of your spell crits (was heals only).  
-   Buffs 
    Fire Resistance Totem :  Also covers the whole raid. Not in Blizzard’s notes, found in the game.   
-   Nerfs 
    Fire Nova :  Much lower base damage and mana cost, and 14.3% of spell power (was 21.4%).  
-   Changes 
    Grounding Totem :  Is destroyed after redirecting a spell. Totemic Recall costs 22 silver at the trainer (was 70).  
-   Changes 
    Elemental Fury :  Needs Lightning Overload, which needs Elemental Alacrity. Elemental Alacrity moves to row 2, Elemental Warding to row 3, Earthbound becomes the 16-point gold medal talent on row 4, and Call of Thunder is removed.  
-   Changes 
    Stormstrike :  Costs 8% of base mana (was 125 at every level). Its debuff is no longer spent by a spell that misses, or by other players and pets.  
-   Changes 
    Lava Burst :  Its first rank is learned at level 30 (was 40) and costs 135 mana (was 165), for 114 to 145 damage (was 150 to 192). Not in Blizzard’s notes, found in the game.   
-   Changes 
    Lava Burst  moved :  Moves from row 7 to row 5 as the 21-point gold medal talent, with no prerequisite and a new rank 4.  
-   Changes 
    Tidal Focus  moved :  Moves to row 1 and Totemic Focus to row 2.     
##  Mage  
-   Buffs 
    Frost Blast :  New spell at level 24: a 1.5 sec cast that deals damage and slows by 40%, and benefits from every Frostbolt talent. It replaces rank 1 Frostbolt’s use, now that downranking is penalized, with a higher cost and a little more damage.  
-   Buffs 
    Arcane Wrath :  New Arcane 16-point gold medal talent: a 3.5 sec cast with Fireball-like damage, plus spell power equal to 5% of your maximum mana. 6 sec cooldown.  
-   Buffs 
    Heating Up :  Is no longer spent when Presence of Mind is used on Pyroblast, and Fire Blast always adds a charge (a Fire Blast crit gives 2).  
-   Buffs 
    Critical Mass :  Goes to 5 points, 10% critical chance in all.  
-   Buffs 
    Frost Channeling :  Lowers your threat as it should.  
-   Buffs 
    Frostbite :  Can trigger from Ice Armor and Frost Armor, as can Fingers of Frost.  
-   Buffs 
    Cone of Cold :  Slows its first 4 targets by 10% more, for 3 sec longer.  
-   Nerfs 
    Arcane Concentration :  Its Clearcasting no longer rolls once per target hit, triggers from Dampen or Amplify Magic, or triggers out of combat.  
-   Nerfs 
    Ice Lance :  Deals 200% more to frozen targets (was 300%).  
-   Changes 
    Arcane Blast :  Is redesigned: a 1.5 sec cast; each cast adds spell damage equal to 15% of your Intellect and raises its cost, up to 4 times, for 15 sec from the first stack. Same mana cost, but damage and spell damage coefficient 40% lower.  
-   Changes 
    Presence of Mind :  Is spent by spell casts, and Missile Barrage no longer lowers the cost and duration of a second Arcane Missiles.  
-   Changes 
    Improved Fire Blast :  Wake of Fire becomes Improved Fire Blast again: no bonus on kills, and Fire Blast gets 8/15 yards more range.  
-   Changes 
    Fire Power :  Is redesigned, 3 points: 1/2/3% more Fire damage for each of your Fire effects on the target (Ignite, Improved Scorch, Fireball, Pyroblast, Impact), up to 12%. Frostfire Bolt benefits but doesn’t count.  
-   Changes 
    Winter's Chill :  Redesigned: no more critical chance. Frostbolt, Frost Blast and Fingers of Frost Ice Lances have a 20/40/60/80/100% chance to apply it, up to 5 stacks, each raising your next Cone of Cold’s damage by 5% and its spell damage by 7%, spent by that Cone of Cold.  
-   Changes 
    Ice Block :  Is less transparent.  
-   Buffs 
    Missile Barrage  moved :  Moves to row 1 as a 2-point talent, and Arcane Missiles hits can trigger it.     
##  Warlock  
-   Buffs 
    Shadow Mastery :  Raises Shadow damage by 2/4/6/8/10% (was 1/2/3/4/5%).  
-   Buffs 
    Soul Link :  Can be cast on your pet again.  
-   Buffs 
    Amplify Curse :  Raises your next Curse of Exhaustion by 50% (was 20%). Not in Blizzard’s notes, found in the game.   
-   Nerfs 
    Summon Incubus :  Is no longer free of Soul Shards with the Reagent Economy Legacy perk, and Curse of Recklessness can no longer stack twice on a target.  
-   Nerfs 
    Portal of Summoning :  Costs 100% of base mana (was 12%) and has a 1 hour cooldown (was 2 min). Not in Blizzard’s notes, found in the game.   
-   Changes 
    Grim Reach :  Replaces Malediction: 10/20% more range for Affliction spells.  
-   Changes 
    Destructive Reach :  10/20% more range for Destruction spells.     
##  Druid  
-   Buffs 
    Faerie Fire :  Generates threat in Bear and Dire Bear Form (it only started combat), and can no longer be on a target twice.  
-   Buffs 
    Shred :  Rank 1 is learned at level 20 (was 22).  
-   Buffs 
    Furor :  Keeps the Energy you had in Cat Form through Bear and Dire Bear Form (Energy still doesn’t regenerate in them).  
-   Nerfs 
    Omen of Clarity :  Can no longer trigger from Faerie Fire in Cat, Bear or Dire Bear Form.  
-   Changes 
    Rejuvenation :  A high rank no longer stops other druids’ Rejuvenation.  
-   Changes 
    Heart of the Wild :  Applies again after logging in, as do Feral Swiftness, Thick Hide and Leader of the Pack.  
-   Changes 
    Moonkin Form :  Its aura now only raises spell critical chance, and covers the whole raid. Several Insect Swarms no longer stack their hit reduction.  
-   Changes 
    Leader of the Pack :  Now only raises melee and ranged critical chance, and covers the whole raid.  
-   Changes 
    Shred :  Its tooltip now counts Savage Fury in its flat damage (the damage was already right).     
##  Human  
-   Changes 
    Ruthless :  Replaces Sword Specialization: 2% critical strike chance.     
##  Orc  
-   Nerfs 
    Blood Fury :  Cooldown rises to 3 min (was 2).  
-   Changes 
    Savage :  Replaces Axe Specialization: 1% critical strike chance.  
-   Changes 
    Shatter Curse :  Now dispels Curses and Banes (it gave immunity), and lowers magic damage taken by 5% (was 15%).     
##  Dwarf  
-   Buffs 
    Find Treasure :  Also gives a chance at extra items in most chests.  
-   Changes 
    Earth Fury :  Replaces Mace Specialization: 2% more critical damage and healing.     
##  Night Elf  
-   Buffs 
    Quickness :  Stacks with other movement speed bonuses. Night Elves no longer show their shield in Wisp Form.     
##  Undead  
-   Nerfs 
    Will of the Forsaken :  Can’t be used under Scatter Shot.  
-   Changes 
    Touch of the Grave :  Procs more evenly: a 30% chance for casters and 20% for Paladins, Rogues and Warriors (Blizzard’s notes call it Touch of the Dead), with a 25 sec internal cooldown. It no longer scales with Shadow damage bonuses, and no longer loses power with a lower spell rank.     
##  Tauren  
-   Buffs 
    Cultivation :  Cooldown drops to 15 min (was 60).  
-   Buffs 
    War Stomp :  Cooldown drops to 90 sec (was 2 min).  
-   Changes 
    Endurance :  Gives 2% more critical damage and healing (was 1% hit chance).  
-   Changes 
    Plainsrunning :  Also gives 3% mounted speed, which stacks with other mount speed bonuses. It now needs you to keep moving a minimum distance each second: stutter-stepping no longer keeps it up.     
##  Gnome  
-   Changes 
    Eureka! :  Becomes an 8 sec buff with no charges, with the same effect on damage and costs, and now also works with Power Word: Shield.     
##  Troll  
-   Buffs 
    Regeneration :  Raises health regeneration by 20% (was 10%).     
##  Skyborne  
-   Buffs 
    Wind Blessed :  Gives 3% haste (was 1%).  
-   Buffs 
    Skysight :  Can be cast while moving.  
-   Buffs 
    Read Leyline :  Can be cast while moving and lasts at least 30 sec (was 20).  
-   Buffs 
    Walk on Air :  Cooldown drops to 90 sec (was 2 min).  
-   Buffs 
    Elemental Insight :  More Elemental Convergences and Leylines spawn in most zones of the Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor.

The level cap rises to 30. The Excavation Site opens in the Wetlands (levels 26 to 31), and Razorfen Downs (25+) and Uldaman (30+) join the dungeons already open.  Blizzard’s notes  also announce: half the bonus experience from dungeon quests, PvP gear costing about 50% more Honor, with the cap raised to 25,000, 100% more Rage from a warrior’s critical strikes (75% for bears), Aggressive mode back for hunter and warlock pets, and barbershops in Darnassus and Thunder Bluff. Last, a BoE item whose appearance you have not collected can no longer go to your other characters once sold to a vendor.

    Call of Thunder :  Now needs Elemental Alacrity instead of Elemental Fury.  
-   Changes 
    Elemental Alacrity  moved :  Moves up to tier 3 and no longer needs Call of Thunder.  
-   Changes 
    Elemental Fury  moved :  Moves down to tier 6 and now needs Call of Thunder.        Mage   
-   Buffs 
    Hot Streak :  Lasts 20 sec instead of 15.  
-   Buffs 
    Wake of Fire :  The bonus to your next Fire Blast lasts 30 sec instead of 20.  
-   Buffs 
    Arcane Missiles :  Line of sight is checked once, when the channel starts: a target that moves out of sight keeps taking the missiles.  
-   Nerfs 
    Ignite :  No longer counts damage bonuses twice.        Druid   
-   Buffs 
    Wrath :  About 50% more damage at every rank.  
-   Buffs 
    Rejuvenation :  Can now land critical hits, as can Tranquility and Wild Growth.  
-   Buffs 
    Thorns :  Now grows with your spell power.  
-   Changes 
    Primal Bite :  The new name of Mangle, which loses its debuff. Not yet translated in the game.  
-   Changes 
    Blood Frenzy :  The new name of Primal Fury, same effect. Not yet translated in the game.        Night Elf   
-   Buffs 
    Starshards :  Twilight Focus now protects it from spell pushback, as it should.        Undead   
-   Changes 
    Touch of the Grave :  No longer breaks crowd control, and only triggers from spells and abilities that deal damage: Shadow Word: Pain on its cast but not its ticks, never Distract or Pick Pocket.  
-   Changes 
    Dark Sacrifice :  No longer breaks crowd control that breaks on damage. Its tooltip now says the mana grows with Spirit, which it already did.        Tauren   
-   Nerfs 
    Cultivation :  Now asks for a player level: the herb’s Herbalism skill divided by 5, and level 60 for herbs above 300 skill.        Gnome   
-   Buffs 
    Contingency Plan :  Can now land critical hits.  
-   Nerfs 
    Eureka! :  Your next 3 abilities now cost 10% less for every class. It used to be 50% for Mages and Warlocks, 40% for Warriors, 20% for Rogues and 15% for Priests. The 10% damage bonus is unchanged.
