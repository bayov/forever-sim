## How to get beta access  
## Opt in for free 
Invitation not guaranteed
On the  official Forever site , top right, Beta Opt-in. Blizzard invites testers from that pool in waves; no guarantee.
## Buy a pack 
Access from the first day
The Skyborne Epic Pack and the Warcraft Forever Collection include beta access from day one, plus the Skyborne race and a name reservation, per  Blizzard’s pre-purchase article . The Heroic Pack unlocks the Skyborne without beta access.
 Once invited,   the beta client shows up in the Battle.net app under World of Warcraft, like the Classic betas did.
## What is in the beta  
The beta covers the leveling game, as the Classic betas did: the reworked talent trees and spellbooks of all nine classes, the new race and class combinations (Undead Paladin, Dwarf Shaman, Human Hunter, Gnome Priest, Orc Mage, Troll Warlock), the Skyborne with their starting zone Zephras Isle, the Legacy system, and the new zones. The level cap was 20 when it opened and has been 30 since October 1.
-     Talent trees and spellbooks  The nine classes, talent by talent and rank by rank    
-     The changes class by class  What moved since Classic, for each class    
-     New race and class combinations  Undead Paladin, Dwarf Shaman, Human Hunter, Gnome Priest, Orc Mage, Troll Warlock    
-     The Skyborne and Zephras Isle  The new race and its starting zone    
-     Four new dungeons  Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, the Excavation Site and the City of Dalaran, all four open under the level 30 cap    
-     Legacy perks  Points shared across your account, spent in three trees

## Questions    How long is the WoW Forever beta? 
34 days, about five weeks: it opened on September 17, 2026 and ends on October 21, 2026. World of Warcraft: Forever launches two weeks later, on November 4, 2026.
   When did the WoW Forever beta start, and when does it end? 
The beta opened on September 17, 2026 and runs until October 21, 2026. World of Warcraft: Forever launches on November 4, 2026.
   How do I get into the WoW Forever beta? 
Two ways. Opt in on the official World of Warcraft: Forever site (top right, "Beta Opt-in"); Blizzard picks testers from that pool, so opting in does not guarantee an invitation. Or buy the Skyborne Epic Pack or the Warcraft Forever Collection, which include beta access from the first day, per Blizzard's pre-purchase article.
   Do I need to buy anything to play WoW Forever? 
No. Forever comes with a World of Warcraft subscription, like Classic. The paid packs add the Skyborne race, beta access, name reservation and cosmetics.
   What does this site do during the beta? 
It reads the talent trees and the class spellbooks from the beta client and compares each talent and spell with the Classic Era client. What was read off the BlizzCon footage for those gave way to the client data.
