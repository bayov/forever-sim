November 3, 2026
Hotfix
Try these changes in the talent tree: 
Hunter
.
A hotfix to the November 2 build, first seen on November 3 in the cache the launch realms send, which Blizzard has posted no notes for. It raises the hunter’s damage: Aspect of the Hawk gets back the ranged attack power the October 28 hotfix took away, Trueshot Aura gives up to twice as much, Barrage and Lightning Reflexes are back to Classic’s values, and Aimed Shot, Arcane Shot, Serpent Sting and Sniper Shot deal more damage.

November 2, 2026
Build 1.60.1.70753
Current build
The November 2 build writes the October 30 hotfix into the client files: the druid and paladin trees’ rework, the racial pass and the mage, priest, warlock and hunter changes now read from the client in every language. New in it: Poised, a passive for rogues at level 18 and druids at level 32 that takes 10 Energy off your first ability when you open a fight at full Energy; the Skyborne’s Attuned and Charged last 1 hr instead of 15 min, as Blizzard announced; the last rank of Holy Shield gets its level, cost and cooldown; and the Pouch of Violet Dust, an item for the launch celebration. None of this is confirmed: the build came while the realms are closed before launch, Blizzard has posted no notes for it, and nothing can be checked in game yet.

Items and Blizzard’s other words
Items
1 new items
One new item: the Pouch of Violet Dust, a junk item for the WoW: Forever launch celebration that you throw into a campfire to transform its flames, with a 30 min cooldown. The other items the build adds were already on the site from the server’s hotfix cache of October 31, but the client now says which recipes their plans, patterns and formulas teach and at what skill, 5 to 20 points earlier than the leveling guides had them: the chain shirts at 80 Blacksmithing, the chain leggings at 95 and the chain helms at 125, Shining Mithril Helm at 215 and Prefect’s Waistguard at 250; the leather armor at 75 Leatherworking, the leather pants at 100 and the leather helms at 125; and the three Heart of the Mountain formulas at 265 Enchanting. Enchant Bracer - Superior Deflection gives 9 Defense instead of 6. A new enchant, Enchant Boots - Speed, gives boots 8% movement speed: its formula is learned at 280 Enchanting, and the enchant takes 2 Large Radiant Shards, 8 Dream Dust, 2 Greater Nether Essences and a Blue Sapphire.
