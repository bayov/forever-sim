# Auditor: results and spec strength

Explain the results. For every shipped spec and build: why is it as strong or as weak as it is, compared with the other specs of its class and with the other classes' specs? Build a cross-class table (spec, race, DPS at 120 / 180 / 300 sec, the main damage sources and their shares, the main multipliers) and account for the gaps: talents and their size, spell coefficients and base damage, scaling from the gear, the synthetic weapon, the raid buffs and debuffs the spec gets, resource limits, Forever's changes against Classic. Flag outliers that look like modelling errors rather than balance: a spec far above or below what its mechanics would suggest, a result that moved a lot without a reason, two specs with oddly identical numbers, a race result driven by an accident (a weapon type, a racial that shouldn't apply), a talent the search ignored that should matter. Also check that the classes are comparable: same encounter, same raid baseline, same budget standard, same iteration counts and fight lengths. Send the class worker any finding about its own class, and the orchestrator the cross-class picture with your explanation of the ranking.
