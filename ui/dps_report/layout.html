        How these numbers were made

        Every level 60 preset was simmed with the same build of the sim. Each preset uses its own talents, rotation, synthetic Phase 1 gear (built to the rules below), consumes and the shared raid buffs and debuffs, on its preset race.

        The main number is the mean of — fights. A fight lasts — s on average, varying by up to — s either way, against a level — boss of no creature type (a generic boss) with — armor before debuffs. Every preset uses the same random seed.

        The race sweep runs — fights on every race the class can be. The fight-length runs use — fights each. The three sample fights are single fights whose DPS is closest to the preset's 10th, 50th and 90th percentile, out of the first — seeds.

        The race scenarios on the Races page use as many fights as the race sweep and the same seed. "Racials off" leaves every racial passive and ability out, so what remains is the race's base stats; turning racials off one at a time splits the rest between them (where two count, each gets the average of its two possible orders, so the parts add up). "Racial weapon" gives Humans a sword, Dwarves a mace and Orcs an axe where the class can use one, with the preset weapon's stats and weapon DPS and the speed of a Phase 1 weapon of that type (Spinal Reaper, Earthshaker, Bonereaver's Edge, Deathbringer, Ironfoe, Annihilator, Hammer of the Vesper, Brutality Blade). The target scenarios change only the boss's creature type.

        Spell names come from the sim's own aura labels, the Forever client talent data and the item database.
