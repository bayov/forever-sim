Forever beta test results, from 2026-10-10
=========================================

Every result from the beta verification session (beta_handoff.md), with the raw data. The
results before 2026-10-10 are in beta_log.txt on master (`jj file show -r master
beta_log.txt`), and the way to read hits is the same as there:
- Each number is the whole hit: the shown amount plus the overkill.
- Crits are listed apart, at their full value. Melee crits are 2x a normal hit and spell
  crits 1.5x.
- Glancing blows are left out.


2026-10-10  Flametongue Totem and melee abilities (item 1)
---------------------------------------------------------
Client 1.60.1.70291 and 1.60.1.70338 (wago.tools SpellEffect and SpellAuraOptions)
  Flametongue Totem's buff (8230, 8250, 10521, 15036) is a party aura that triggers the
  proc spell (aura 42), with ProcChance 100, no proc cooldown and ProcTypeMask 0x4 (white
  melee swings only).
  Windfury Weapon (439431) and Flametongue Weapon (436519) have mask 0x14 (white swings and
  melee abilities).
  The Era client (1.15.x, 70003) still has the old form: Flametongue Totem enchants the
  held item (effect 92), and the server decides which hits trigger it.

Beta
  The user saw that Stormstrike never procs Flametongue Totem, and remembers only one
  Flametongue Totem hit per Windfury proc.

What it showed: Flametongue Totem procs on white swings only. The audit session changed
the sim (5229ae79). Windfury Weapon's own two attacks weren't counted apart, so the item 2
run checks them on the side.
