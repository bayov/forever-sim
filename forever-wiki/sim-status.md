# Sim status

What this repo's sim models of Forever as of 2026-09-20, and what it assumes where nothing is published. Racials and the Rogue, Shaman and Paladin trees follow the beta client (build 1.60.1.69876), the rest is still the BlizzCon transcriptions. Most of the engine work was ported from [ElliotWood/Forever](https://github.com/ElliotWood/Forever) (see [references.md](references.md)), then checked against this wiki and adjusted. Only Warrior, Rogue, Shaman and Paladin have Forever talent trees so far. The other five classes still run their Classic trees.

## Ruleset switch

`SimOptions.ruleset` picks `RulesetForever` (the wire default, so anything that never sets it runs Forever) or `RulesetClassic`. The web UI has a "Forever Rules" checkbox in the options menu (cog icon in the top bar). The unconverted classes pin their tests to Classic. Everything below is gated on Forever, so Classic results are unchanged. The Warrior, Rogue, Shaman and Paladin trees are Forever only either way, because the talent proto is positional and cannot hold two trees.

## Core mechanics

| Rule | Modelled as | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unified hit | Hit printed on gear counts for melee, ranged and spells. Hit from talents and buffs is untouched. | Deep Dive panel |
| Unified crit | Crit printed on gear counts for melee, ranged and spells. Agility and Intellect conversions are untouched. | Deep Dive panel |
| Bonus healing | Nothing extra. Forever's healing gear prints its damage part on the tooltip ("healing by up to 21 and damage by up to 7"), and the item data already has it as spell power plus healing. | Item tooltips |
| Weapon skill | Still Classic. Forever thins it out on gear, but the Forever items seen so far (low level quest rewards) carry none. | Deep Dive panel, no numbers |
| Avoidance reduction stat | Not modelled. No item carries it. | Deep Dive panel |
| Periodic crits | Dot ticks roll against the caster's crit chance by school. | ElliotWood/Forever's reading, not confirmed by Blizzard |
| Stormstrike | Marks the target for the shaman's next Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning or Earth Shock (+20%, 12 sec). One charge, the shaman's own, so the external Stormstrike debuff does nothing under Forever. 125 Mana, 8 sec cooldown. | Beta client tooltip |
| Personal debuffs | Improved Shadow Bolt, Shadow Weaving, Improved Scorch and Winter's Chill are no longer raid debuffs in the test suites and presets. | Talent transcriptions |
| Windfury | The sim already models Windfury Totem as a main hand only proc that cannot be combined with a shaman's own main hand Windfury Weapon, which is what Forever says. | Spellbook transcription |
| Faction buffs | Paladin blessings and Shaman totems (including Windfury Totem) apply to every race. Classic gated blessings to Alliance and totems to Horde. | Dwarf Shaman and Undead Paladin exist, so both factions have both classes |
| Judgement | Does not consume the seal, so a paladin keeps one seal up and judges it every 10 sec (8 with Improved Judgement). Judgement of the Crusader lasts 40 sec, the paladin's melee hits refresh it, and it carries the old Improved Seal of the Crusader value (23 / 35 / 58 / 92 / 127 / 161 by rank, the raid debuff is 161 whether or not it says improved). Judging Righteousness or Command while it is up leaves it up. | Wowhead's Forever spell database |
| Paladin spells | Holy Strike is baseline: 25 / 29 / 32 / 36 / 39 / 43 / 46 / 50% weapon damage plus 12 / 17 / 19 / 24 / 31 / 53 / 68 / 93 Holy (growing a little per level after each rank), 10 sec cooldown, Holy school so it ignores armor and gives no seal procs. Consecration is baseline (no talent) and reworked: everyone in it takes a quarter of Classic's damage, the first four enemies another 233 / 384 of it (the BlizzCon slide's rank 5, 96 plus 233, the other ranks take the same ratio), with a 9.5% spell power coefficient per tick on the extra part, and every sim target counts as one of the four. Retribution Aura is 7 / 12 / 18 / 24 / 30. Righteous Fury is 90% threat. Holy Shield blocks 20% more and hits for 110 / 153 / 221, rank 1 at 40. Holy Shock is a level 30 talent (129 to 139, 160 mana) with trainer ranks behind it (175 / 248 / 334 at the low end, Classic 204 / 279 / 365) and a 10 sec cooldown. Lay on Hands is 20 min. Sanctity Aura is gone, so the paladin's own aura option is Retribution Aura. Seal of Righteousness procs, Seal of the Crusader's attack power and Seal of Command are unchanged, and so are the judgements of Righteousness and Command. Exorcism, Hammer of Wrath (286 / 382 / 489 at level 60) and Holy Wrath have Forever's damage. Seal of Fury (the tanking seal), Twist of Light and Light's Vigil are not modelled. | Wowhead's Forever spell database, diffed against Classic for every paladin spell on 2026-09-20 |
| Spell values | Rupture does about 60% of its Classic damage (469 over 16 sec at 5 points for rank 6, Classic 800). Expose Armor takes more armor per point (450 at rank 5, Classic 340, or 425 with the old 2/2 talent, and Improved Expose Armor no longer raises it). Lightning Bolt ranks 4 and up cast in 2.5 sec (Classic 3) and Chain Lightning in 2 (Classic 2.5), both about 20% cheaper. Weapon imbues last an hour, Windfury Weapon rank 4 gives 433 attack power (Classic 333) and Rockbiter rank 7 686 (Classic 653). Totems last 5 min and reach 30 yards. Windfury Totem gives 95 / 179 / 246 (Classic 122 / 229 / 315), Strength of Earth 7 / 14 / 25 / 42 / 53 (Classic 10 / 20 / 36 / 61 / 77), Grace of Air 49 / 77 / 89 (Classic 43 / 67 / 77). Backstab's bonus damage was already the Forever two thirds. | Wowhead's Forever spell database (beta 1.60.1 client data, `nether.wowhead.com/forever/tooltip/spell/<id>?dataEnv=17`), diffed against Classic for every rogue and shaman spell on 2026-09-19. The shocks, Lightning Bolt and the totems have Forever values since (see the 2026-09-24 and 2026-10-01 notes below). Eviscerate only changed at rank 6 (71 per combo point, Classic 77). |

`sim/core/racials_forever.go`, written against [racials.md](racials.md), which carries the beta client's tooltips and cooldowns. Blood Fury and Eureka! are 2 min, Berserking and Elune's Light 3 min.

## Talents

Trees for Rogue, Shaman and Paladin come from the beta client (build 1.60.1.69876, read through hyjal.cc's talent calculator and vendored in `tools/forever_talents/client/`, converted with `import_client.py`). Every rank is real. The changes against the BlizzCon data are listed at the top of [classes/rogue.md](classes/rogue.md), [classes/shaman.md](classes/shaman.md) and [classes/paladin.md](classes/paladin.md). The Warrior tree is still `tools/forever_talents/data/warrior.json` from the nikftw transcription (rank 1 observed, the rest extrapolated), the client tree for it and the other five classes sits in `client/` waiting for the conversion.

Regenerate a tree and its proto with `tools/forever_talents/import_talents.py <class> --write` (after `import_client.py <class>` for a class on the client data). The tree json and the proto message have to stay in the same order, so always regenerate both.

The talent picker shows the Forever tooltip for every talent and marks two things:

- Ranks 2 and up that were never on screen and are extrapolated (Warrior only now).
- Talents the sim does not read, outlined in the picker. Points spent there change nothing. As of now: Warrior (Improved Charge, Spearing Strike, Improved Hamstring, Booming Voice, Iron Will, Piercing Howl, Blood Crazed, Improved Intercept, Improved Disarm, Vanguard, Improved Shield Bash), Rogue (Improved Gouge, Remorseless Attacks, Improved Kidney Shot, Improved Sprint, Improved Kick, Camouflage, Master of Deception, Setup, Dirty Tricks, Improved Distract, Heightened Senses), Shaman (Eye of the Storm, Elemental Reach, Earthbound, Earth's Grasp, Improved Ghost Wolf, Improved Healing Wave, Ancestral Healing, Healing Focus, Healing Way, Riptide), Paladin (Healing Light, Spiritual Focus, Unyielding Faith, Voice of Truth, Infusion of Light, Light's Vigil, Guardian's Favor, Improved Seal of Fury, Swift Judgement, Improved Hammer of Justice, Templar's Bulwark, Pursuit of Justice, Eye for an Eye, Twist of Light).

Per-class assumptions carried over from the fork are marked `TODO` in `sim/warrior`, `sim/rogue`, `sim/shaman` and `sim/paladin`. The ones that move DPS:

- Rogue: the client settled Hack and Slash, Serrated Blades, Puncturing Wounds and Lethality (4% per rank, Classic's 6% was a 50 DPS overestimate at 5/5, and the searched builds now take Murder 2 over the last two Lethality points: Sinister Strike `00532310301-32003311201515231` 803 DPS, Backstab `005323102-30230320201515231-002` 892 on the Phase 2 sets against the level 60 raid setup in `tools/rotopt/mksettings`, down from 843 and 936 before the wowhead spell data cut Rupture and the Strength of Earth and Windfury totems). Flawless Execution (Eviscerate 25 Energy) is in both builds. Mutilate costs 60 Energy, needs daggers and adds 17.25 per weapon. Venom costs 25 Energy, and as transcribed (30% poison damage, no damage of its own) it is a 3 to 5% DPS loss at any combo point count because the points are worth more as Eviscerate. Improved Expose Armor only discounts the finisher, the Forever ranks carry the bigger armor value (450 per point at rank 5). Neither level 60 build uses Rupture, so its cut costs them nothing directly, the lost 40 DPS is the raid's Strength of Earth and Windfury Totem.
- Paladin: converted on 2026-09-20 for the level 20 retribution question, the level 60 presets are still blank gear with a hand made talent build. Improved Seals scales every seal proc and judgement, Crusade multiplies the attack tables like the creature racials (so it double dips on crits against Demons and Undead), Vengeance stacks 1% per point per crit to 5, Vindication is its 1% per point of attack power (the target's loss is not scored), Reckoning procs on 20% per point of crits taken and 8% per point of blocks, Redoubt on 10% of melee hits taken, Consecrated Ground is a paladin Holy damage buff for Consecration's 8 sec, Reverence is casting regen, Sanctified Judgement refunds the seal's cost, Holy Conduit and Benediction discount their spells, Purifying Power and Instrument of Law shorten Exorcism, Holy Wrath and Hammer of Wrath. Lay on Hands no longer fires itself under 10% health: the UI always sets a healing model, so a paladin taking hits dumped its whole pool on it. Seal of Command needs its talent now (the fork registered it for everyone).
- Shaman: Three enhancement rotations ship. "Optimized" (the default) is the tools/rotopt search result: Flame Shock when its DoT is down and Earth Shock otherwise, Lightning Bolt only at 5 Maelstrom Weapon stacks, Rage of the Farseer on cooldown with Blood Fury and Berserking waiting for it when the wait costs no use (it never does on a 2 min Blood Fury against a 3 min Rage in a 4 to 5 min fight, so the holds change nothing). "Grace of Air" holds Grace of Air, "Windfury Totem" holds Windfury Totem for the melee group. A "Twist WF + GoA" rotation was dropped on 2026-09-25, because the 2026-09-24 beta build stopped the air totem buffs from stacking in a party. The sim gives the shaman nothing for its own Windfury Totem (Windfury Weapon does not stack with it), so with no second shaman in the group the player's own Strength of Earth is worth about 25 DPS and Grace of Air about 33 (one cast each now that totems last 5 min), and Windfury Totem in place of Grace of Air costs those 33. The twisting number (about 160 with 2 min totems) predates the 5 min totems. On the Phase 2 gear with the searched talents the Optimized rotation measures about 934 DPS against a Humanoid in the default raid (both blessings, Judgement of Wisdom, every debuff, no second shaman). It was 860 before the beta client put Stormstrike on an 8 sec cooldown and 960 before the wowhead spell data took Strength of Earth to 53 (the totems now last 5 min, so each is one cast per fight). The knob search does not move with the new values. Magma Totem now edges Searing by 6. The Level 60 talents (`0505331-055030031005112251` since the 2026-09-24 build moved Elemental Fury out of reach, 821 DPS where the old build was 834, with the one spare point in Elemental Focus for 420 more mana at the end of a 300 sec fight) pin Spirit Weapons (30% less threat, which the sim does not score but a raid needs) with `tools/rotopt -require spiritWeapons`, and it costs under 1 DPS: Reverberation 3 and Call of Flame 3 tie Reverberation 5 and Call of Flame 2 with the 8 sec Stormstrike and Magma Totem. Judgement of Wisdom is the shaman's mana: without it the pool is dry two minutes in and the rotation loses 60 DPS. With no Mana Spring the pool is dry at five minutes anyway, so the Level 60 talents take Improved Stormstrike over the last two points of Ancestral Knowledge (2 DPS behind in a 4 minute fight, 6 ahead at 8 minutes, 12 at 10). Improved Stormstrike's regen was a no-op in the fork (the mana tick was never recomputed) and is fixed. Windfury Weapon is 100 DPS ahead of Rockbiter and 140 ahead of Flametongue on Sulfuras. A two hander beats any one hander plus shield by 60 or more. Twisting also spends two thirds of the mana pool on totems and runs the shaman dry by 85 sec of a 2 min fight. Maelstrom Weapon has no published proc rate, modelled as 2 PPM per point. Rage of the Farseer assumes a 3 min cooldown. Lava Burst cast time and cooldown are from the WotLK spell. Enhancing Totems is gone so Strength of Earth and Grace of Air always land improved. A "Level 60 + Imp GW" talent preset pins Improved Ghost Wolf 2 as well (`-require spiritWeapons,improvedGhostWolf`): the points come out of Ancestral Knowledge, the spare point goes back into it (`050533-054032031005112251`), and the build is 1.2 DPS behind at 300 sec. Water Shield (Restoration row 3, so 11 points there) is not a DPS talent: the best build with it, `050013-053030031005112151-050050001`, gives up Elemental Fury, Reverberation and two points of Call of Flame and sits 40 DPS behind at every length (922 against 961 at 5 min, 914 against 953 at 15 min), while the shield itself is worth at most 8 DPS at 15 min with raid damage spending a globe every 5 sec (the most the 3.5 sec globe rate and 15 sec recast allow), 3 at 10 min, nothing at 8 and a loss of 2 to 4 under 5 min because each recast is a GCD. Judgement of Wisdom and Improved Stormstrike keep the base build from running dry enough for the mana to matter. The client's Water Shield is free. Its tooltip said it had a 15 sec cooldown, but the 2026-10-08 beta build notes say it never had one, so the sim has no cooldown on it since then. Nothing hits a raid DPS shaman in the sim, so an enhancement setting "Raid damage hits per minute" (Forever only) spends its globes at a steady rate. Setting the shaman as the boss's tank instead would also hand it Improved Stormstrike's dodge and parry resets and parry haste, worth 85 DPS, which raid damage does not do since spells cannot be dodged.

## Gear and buffs

Gear up to required level 25 is what wowhead's Forever database shows (scraped 2026-09-22 by `tools/database/forever_wowhead/scrape.py` into `assets/db_inputs/forever_wowhead_items.json`, laid over the Classic items by `gen_db`): Forever reworked a lot of low level Classic gear (Spirit became spell power on cloth and on quest rewards like Heartwood Girdle and Serpent Gloves, the Deadmines and Wailing Caverns weapons hit 20 to 40% harder, white crafted pieces turned green, Shadowfang and Cobrahn's Grasp lost a little) and added its own (bind on pickup crafted sets from Blacksmithing, Leatherworking and Tailoring at 15 to 25, Technician's Bracers, Dark Ritual Leggings, Disjointed Shoes). 2754 Classic items are laid over with Forever's stats and 1570 items came in, 1056 of them with no drop, quest, vendor or recipe wowhead knows. Leaving those out cost us Wolfsbane, the two handed sword the Undead paladin quest chain hands over and the best weapon a level 20 paladin can hold, so they all come in now and `rotopt` marks a pick it lists no way of getting for a look by hand. Four of those turned up in a level 20 search and none of them could be confirmed (Mark of the Pack Leader and the rest of the Blessing of Kalimdor set, Dro'zem's Tunic, First Mate Band, Sludge-Stained Band), so the shipped sets leave them out and only hold gear someone can point at. Items above required level 25 are still Classic gear with Classic stats, plus the hand typed Forever items in `tools/database/forever_items.go` (the Ruins of Lordaeron and Hall of Thanes rewards, the Ratchet reputation gear). Rating stats ("+14 Critical Strike", "+10 Hit": 14 crit rating is 1% at 60 on the Dungeon 2 sets, 10 hit rating is 1%, the level 20 scaling is unknown) are dropped from the item, so a level 60 rescrape needs the unit first. Bind on pickup crafted gear and the engineering goggles carry their profession, the UI warns when the character lacks it and `rotopt -professions` picks the pair the gear search may use. Faction comes from the listing row's `side`, which wowhead already works out from the quest or vendor behind the item, so Verigan's Fist, Sparkmetal Coif and Sentinel's Medallion are Alliance and Hammerbone, Screecher Belt and Jutebraid Gloves are Horde. A quest only one class can take is the other half of that: Forever's Friend of the Library hands a mage Erudite's Amulet, which carries no class restriction of its own. The scrape reads wowhead's quest listing the same way it reads the item listing, and a quest row says which classes and which side can take it, so 52 items get their class from the quest that hands them over (Verigan's Fist, Moonsilver Blade, the Library rewards). When the item and its quest disagree nobody can have it and it stays out of the database: Field Researcher's Loop is marked rogue gear, but only a mage can take Greater Friend of the Library. Forever enchants have no level requirement (Crusader, Spellblasting 65 spell power on a two hander), the level 20 presets carry none. World buffs are all off in the presets, since Forever does not have them at launch. They can still be turned on in the Settings tab. Raid buff assumptions are Classic 40-man ones, Forever's launch raids are 10 and 20 man plus Onyxia at 40. The buffs from classes the sim does not play take Forever's values by the player's level since 2026-10-01 (see the class deep dives bullet under Low levels).

## Low levels

The sim takes a player level (Other settings) with 1.12 base stats and spell ranks per level, and an Extra Talent Points setting next to it. Extra points never take a character past the 51 a level 60 has. Forever hands them out from level 25 and the beta stops at 20, so a level 20 has the 11 points Classic gives it and the setting stays at 0 until the cap moves. The Rogue and Enhancement Shaman UIs ship "Level 20" builds (gear, talents, rotation, race, level and encounter together) from the tools/rotopt searches: every item the level can equip, every build of the damage talents, then the rotation knobs. The encounter is Mutanus the Devourer (level 22, 922 armor), 60 sec, no raid buffs, the boss hitting the shaman so Lightning Shield fires.

- Level 20 (11 points): Sinister Strike 63 DPS, Backstab 61, Enhancement 63 (Rockbiter, Frost Shock, Searing Totem, Strength of Earth, Lightning Shield), Retribution 63.6 (Dwarf, see below).
