# Overview

## What Forever is

Announced at BlizzCon 2026 (September 12). A third permanent version of WoW next to Modern and Classic, not a Classic offshoot. It is the rumored "Classic+". Included with any active WoW subscription. The Skyborne race is tied to a paid edition.

- Set in the first year of the original game. After the Third War, before the Dark Portal reopens. It runs as its own continuity beside retail and Classic Era.
- Level cap is 60 with no plan to raise it. Progression comes from new zones, items, reputations, professions and builds.
- No flying, no level scaling, no expansion cycle. Gear does not get invalidated by the next patch.
- Four design pillars: approachable and familiar, the world as the main character, the journey before the destination, protect the social experience.
- Stated class goal: all 27 specs viable in dungeons and raids. Viable, not identical. Specs keep clear strengths and weaknesses.

## Dates

- Beta: 2026-09-17 to 2026-10-22, level cap 30 in beta.
- Name reservation and character creation: 2026-10-27.
- Launch: 2026-11-04 at 3:00 p.m. PST, global.
- Raids unlock: 2026-12-09 (Barrow Deeps 10 player, Hyjal Summit 20 player, Onyxia's Lair 40 player).
- Hardcore ruleset: winter 2026, after launch.
- Spring 2027: 2 new raids (10 and 20 player), 2 new dungeons, new quests and playable area, new legendary questline, PvP season refresh.
- Summer 2027: revamped iconic vanilla raid, a new raid, expanded world content, 2 new dungeons, PvP season refresh, professions and Legacy updates.

## Rulesets instead of realms

No realm list. You pick a ruleset and land in one very large shared world.

- Normal: world PvP only if you opt in.
- PvP: contested zones stay dangerous. Uses the faction balance tools from Season of Discovery.
- Roleplaying.
- Hardcore: permanent death, arrives after launch. Sealed ecosystem, progress and collections cannot be carried in. A dead Hardcore character can move to another ruleset, including PvP.

Factions stay apart. No cross-faction grouping. Groups must come from the same ruleset. Language preference matching. Characters have two-part names (first and last), unique across the region.

## Zones

- Zephras Isle (1 to 12): Skyborne starting zone. Floating island held up by air elementals.
- Riverglades (mid 30s to mid 40s): roughly Stranglethorn-sized, east coast between Redridge, Burning Steppes, Badlands and Swamp of Sorrows. 150 to 200 quests. Horde start near Ragmar, Alliance at Farhold Keep.
- Shen'dralas: new region south of Desolace (between Mulgore and Desolace). Shen'dralar history, Eldre'Thalas, Prince Tortheldrin, Gelkis and Magram centaurs.
- Mount Hyjal: the endgame zone. Regrown after Archimonde's defeat. New factions, reputations, solo and group content. Darkwhisper Gorge.
- Dalaran returns rebuilt in the Alterac Mountains (not floating), with its barrier just failed.
- Old zones get dozens of new quests each. Wetlands gets 25+ quests and a new route, Desolace gets centaur faction content, Azshara gets a serious expansion.
- Three new ship routes: Stormwind to Auberdine, Menethil to Southshore to Auberdine, Steamwheedle Port (Tanaris) to somewhere new.
- Over 1,000 new quests spread across 1 to 60.

How dungeons play, per Blizzard: an ordinary mob takes 10 to 15 seconds to kill solo. Crowd control matters again. Tanks are built to hold three or four enemies, not a room. Consecration is the example: light damage to everything, heavy damage only to the first four targets. Dungeon boss loot was re-examined, unique boss items are now blue quality, set bonuses improved, hybrids get gear made for them.

## Raids

- Barrow Deeps, 10 player. Night elven barrow dens with three entrances (one on Hyjal). Corruption spreading.
- Hyjal Summit, 20 player. The larger launch raid. Story about what Malfurion gave up to stop Archimonde. Looks like the route to Forever's first legendary.
- Onyxia's Lair, 40 player, opens with them on 2026-12-09.
- The vanilla raids exist in the timeline but have not opened yet. Forever is set before Molten Core. A revamped iconic raid is pencilled in for summer 2027.

This matters for buffs in a sim: launch raids are 10 and 20 player, so a full 40-man buff set is only right for Onyxia.

## The Skyborne

New elven race, the Shandori. Descended from Highborne who stayed in Eldre'Thalas after the War of the Ancients and rebelled against Tortheldrin. Spent ten thousand years in the Elemental Plane of Air (Skywall), which changed their look. Their island is failing and pieces are falling away.

- Windshaper Skyborne join the Horde and can be Shaman.
- High Order Skyborne join the Alliance and can be Mage.
- Both: Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Druid. Own druid forms, voices, dances.
- Racials in [racials.md](racials.md).

## PvP

Original battlegrounds stay. New 15 vs 15 battleground on the Darkspear Islands, plays like Eye of the Storm with Arathi Basin style flag captures. A larger PvP system was teased without detail. Resilience is not being added.

## Presentation

Transmog exists and can be disabled for everyone else. Same armor type only. One toggle between original and HD character models. Official gamepad support.

## Teased, not detailed

Grim Batol and red dragons, more Azshara, a revamped iconic raid, the first legendary, a larger PvP system, more race and class combos, possibly a new class.
