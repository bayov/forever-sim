# Stats and gear

Blizzard called this "a deliberate un-solving of the Classic gear meta". These are rules, not numbers. The beta is the first source of Forever item stats: as of 2026-09-19 the low level dungeon quest rewards and reputation gear are visible (wowhead Forever tooltips, wowforevertalents.com), the level 60 reputation gear shows hit and crit as whole numbers that look like ratings, and no raid item has appeared. See [sim-status.md](sim-status.md) for what the sim carries.

## Combat stat changes (from the Deep Dive panel)

- Hit chance is one stat across melee, ranged and spell.
- Critical strike chance is one stat across melee, ranged and spell. Racial weapon specializations follow this: Orc Axe Specialization is "Axes increase spell and ability critical strike chance by 1%", and it applies to spells as well as attacks.
- Weapon skill survives but is spread more thinly across items and "is no longer overwhelming". Weapon skill racials are gone, replaced by crit (see [racials.md](racials.md)).
- A new stat reduces the chance your attacks get dodged or parried (an expertise-like stat). Mostly aimed at tanks.
- Bonus healing on gear now also includes one third as much bonus damage. Healers can quest in one set.
- Caster weapons provide spell damage and healing from around level 10.
- Resilience is not being added.

## Itemization changes

- World-drop epics reworked and new ones added.
- Trinkets pushed toward varied, situational effects rather than universal stat sticks.
- Some gear performs differently by terrain or by enemy family (creature-specific bonuses). Blizzard's intent is that you keep a collection, not one BiS list.
- Quest rewards widened so more builds get something. Examples given: Mor'Ladim also offers a spell-power ring, Nesingwary's questline adds a wand and a Strength plus spell damage sword aimed at Retribution.
- Dungeon drops re-examined. Unique dungeon boss items are now blue quality. Weak drops rebuilt, new items added, iconic pieces left alone. Set bonuses improved.
- Hybrids get dungeon and quest gear made for them.
- Belt buckles from Blacksmithing add "socketed stats" to waist armor (wording from a fan transcription, exact mechanic unknown).
- Ring enchants from Enchanting. Armor kits for leather and mail from Leatherworking. Embroidery on cloth from Tailoring. Alchemy makes an upgradeable trinket (Philosopher's Stone) and longer or stronger elixirs and flasks (Mixology).

## Unknowns that matter for a sim

- Item stat budgets, how much hit, crit, expertise-like stat and weapon skill appear on launch gear.
- Whether hit and crit ratings are percent on gear (as in Classic) or a rating system. Panel wording says "chance", which suggests percent.
- How the unified crit interacts with talents that used to be melee-only or spell-only (for example Thundering Strikes now reads "all spells and attacks").
- Launch gear is pre-raid only. Forever sits before Molten Core in the timeline, so there is no raid loot until 2026-12-09, and even then the raids are Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia, none of which have published loot tables yet.
- Consumables: Alchemy gets Mixology (longer or stronger elixirs and flasks). Nearly every cooked food gives a stat bonus. Camp buffs (see [professions.md](professions.md)) share a category with the equivalent class buff, so an Intellect incense competes with Arcane Intellect rather than stacking.
