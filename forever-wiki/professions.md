# Professions and camping

Source: Blizzard Deep Dive recap and the wowforevertalents.com professions page (transcribed from BlizzCon 2026 footage). Classic had no per-character profession perks, Forever adds them.

## General

- Over 600 new recipes across professions and level ranges. Starting zones get more profession trainers, recipes and related quests.
- Gathering professions get specializations (herbalists toward rare lotus or raw volume, miners toward extra ore or rare materials).
- Nearly every cooked food gives a stat bonus and a small experience trickle.
- Mount costs move to the Burning Crusade split: more on riding training, less on the mount.

## Per-profession perks

Wording is from the fan transcription. Numbers were only shown for a few.

### Alchemy
- Mixology: elixirs and flasks last longer and provide stronger effects.
- Philosopher's Stone: craft an upgradeable trinket that grows with Alchemy skill.
- New recipes beyond Classic.

### Blacksmithing
- Repair All: repair all your equipment at once.
- Belt Buckles: craft belt buckles that add socketed stats to waist armor.
- New recipes beyond Classic.

### Enchanting
- Ring Enchants: permanent combat bonuses on rings.
- Shard Transmutes: transmute shards into higher grades.
- Stave crafting and more recipes.

### Engineering
- Remains one of the most powerful combat professions.
- Additional gadgets, bombs and trinkets beyond Classic.
- Goblin or Gnomish specialization for unique recipes.

### Herbalism
- Natural Talent: resistance to all schools of magic increased.
- Lotus Affinity: better chance to find rare lotus herbs.
- Brutal Pickings: gather more herbs from nodes.

### Leatherworking
- Armor Kits: improve leather and mail gear.
- Must Go Faster: mounted speed increased while Leatherworking is trained.
- Dragonscale and elemental leather sets.

### Mining
- Made of Metal: total health increased by 5%.
- A Lode of Ore: extra ore from nodes.
- Malicious Mining: nodes may yield rare or combat-oriented materials.

### Skinning
- Beasts of the Wild: damage to Beasts and Dragonkin increased by 5%.
- Rare Hides: better chance to skin rare and specialty hides.

### Tailoring
- Embroider Your Work: embroider cloth gear with profession-only bonuses.
- Cloth Scavenging: chance for extra cloth from humanoids.
- New and reworked cloth sets and recipes.

## Sim-relevant takeaways

- Skinning gives a flat 5% vs Beasts and Dragonkin (Onyxia is a Dragonkin).
- Mining gives 5% health.
- Alchemy's Mixology and Philosopher's Stone, Engineering's extra gadgets, Enchanting's ring enchants, Blacksmithing's belt buckles, Leatherworking's armor kits and Tailoring's embroidery all add throughput with unknown numbers.

## Camping

The Cooking Fire becomes a campsite with rest, vendors, repairs, profession workspaces and one-hour buffs. You have to sit by the fire a moment before buffs land.

- A camp holds three crafted objects (five or ten with upgraded Campfires learned by cooks), one per nearby player, each with a one-hour cooldown. Every profession has three objects learned at different skill levels (first around skill 20), better versions from Blueprint recipes that drop from specific dungeon bosses.
- Examples: Blacksmith Sharpening Wheel (Attack Power per Blizzard, Strength per the fan page), Tailor Faction Banner (Spirit), Herbalist Incense Candle (Intellect).
- Camp buffs share a category with the equivalent class buff. The incense competes with Arcane Intellect rather than stacking.
- Legacy perk Permanence makes camp buffs and long-duration class buffs last 50% longer.
