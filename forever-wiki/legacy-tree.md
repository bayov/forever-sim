# Legacy Tree

Account-wide progression next to class talents. Legacy Points (LP) come from ordinary accomplishments: reaching 25, 45 and 60 with a class, profession milestones like Artisan Engineering, challenges while levelling and exploring. The pool is shared across the account but spent per character. At launch you can spend 16 points out of roughly 65 earnable. Points past the cap feed a cosmetic and prestige reward track. Hardcore is a sealed ecosystem and does not share.

None of these perks add combat power. Values below are rank 1 as shown on the level 38 show-floor character (transcribed by wowforevertalents.com). "Unknown" nodes are shown that way in the game too.

## Professions

- Working Overtime (5 ranks): +4% chance to gain a skill increase from using any primary, secondary or class-based tradeskill.
- Bountiful Harvest (5 ranks): discover 20% more Scarce materials from Mining, Herbalism and Skinning.
- Master Chef (5 ranks): cooking recipes have a 10% chance to create an extra result.
- Performance Bonus (3 ranks): 5% chance to receive 100% increased Merchant's Favor when you turn in a crate to the Azeroth Commerce Authority or Durotar Supply and Logistics.
- Luremaster (2 ranks): while fishing with a Lure, 25% chance to catch an extra fish.
- Dedicated Study (1 rank, 25 sec cast, 23 hour cooldown): +1 skill in your lowest primary or secondary tradeskill. If all are at 300, gain 2 to 4 of a random Elemental Essence.
- Two Unknown nodes, plus one node not hovered in the footage.

## Adventure

- Well Rested (5 ranks): rested experience accumulates 4% faster and the cap is 4% higher.
- Talented (5 ranks): gain talent points every level starting at level 9 instead of 10, still capped at 51 total.
- Field Guide (3 ranks): cooldown on adding Camp features reduced by 8%.
- Field Medicine (2 ranks): Recently Bandaged duration reduced by 5 sec. Ineffective in dungeons, raids and battlegrounds.
- Frequent Flier (1 rank): 50% discount on flight paths, flight mount 20% faster.
- Three Unknown nodes.

## Resourcefulness

- Gourmand (3 ranks): duration of food buffs increased by 33%.
- Reinforce (5 ranks): 8% less durability loss on death.
- Reagent Economy (1 rank): class abilities no longer require vendor reagents, Tier 1 camping features cost no reagents.
- Permanence (2 ranks): long-duration party or raid stat buffs from your class last 50% longer, camp rest benefits last 50% longer.
- For Great Honor (5 ranks): Honor gained +2%.
- Two Unknown nodes.
