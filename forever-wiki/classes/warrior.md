# Warrior

Source: wowforevertalents.com dataset (BlizzCon 2026 footage overlaid on Classic Era 1.15.9 client tables), data version 2026-09-13/14. See [../README.md](../README.md) for caveats. Numbers on new talents were read off level 38 demo characters, so absolute damage and mana values are not level 60 values.

Talents: 54 total, 11 new, 31 changed, 12 unchanged (verified).

Row N needs 5*(N-1) points spent in that tree. Row 1 is the top row. Rows 3, 4, 5 and 7 hold the 11, 16, 21 and 31 point talents.

Warning on multi-rank NEW and CHANGED talents: the footage showed rank 1 only. The dataset scaled the other ranks linearly from rank 1, which sometimes produces nonsense (for example Maelstrom Weapon rank 5 reading 'stacks up to 25 times'). Treat rank 1 as observed and the rest as a guess until the beta client is out. The [ranks 2+ unverified] tag marks every multi-rank new or changed talent. Read the Note line, it says when a higher rank was actually seen on a BlizzCon slide.

## Spellbook changes (trainer abilities)

32 of 36 trainer abilities confirmed from footage. Abilities not listed below are either verified unchanged or still Classic placeholders (unverified).

### New abilities

- **Tactical Mastery** (arms, Passive)
  - You retain up to 10 Rage when you change Stances.
  - Note: Baseline Arms passive, the Improved Tactical Mastery talent adds 3 Rage per rank on top. In Classic this was a five-rank talent keeping 5 Rage per rank.
- **Victory Rush** (arms, Melee Range, Instant, 30 sec cooldown)
  - Instantly attack the target causing 133 damage and healing you for 10% of your maximum health. Only useable within 20 sec after you kill a non-trivial enemy.
  - Note: Baseline Arms ability without ranks. The level 38 tauren's tooltip read 133 damage, the damage presumably scales with the character.

### Changed abilities

- **Thunder Clap** (arms, Level 38, 20 Rage, Instant, 4 sec cooldown)
  - Blasts nearby enemies with thunder, increasing the time between their attacks by 20% for 22 sec and doing 55 damage to them. Will affect up to 4 targets.
  - Classic: Blasts nearby enemies with thunder increasing the time between their attacks by 10% for 30 sec and doing 103 damage to them. Will affect up to 4 targets.
  - Note: The attack-speed slow doubled to 20% and the ability is now usable in Defensive Stance as well as Battle Stance. Rank 4 tooltip on a level 38 warrior, damage matches Classic.
- **Retaliation** (arms, Level 20, Instant, 15 min cooldown)
  - Instantly counterattack any enemy that strikes you in melee for 15 sec. Melee attacks made from behind cannot be counterattacked. A maximum of 30 attacks will cause retaliation.
  - Note: Cooldown halved to 15 minutes, the effect text is unchanged.
- **Battle Shout** (fury, Level 32, 10 Rage, Instant)
  - The warrior shouts, increasing the melee attack power of all party members within 20 yards by 54. Lasts 3 min.
  - Classic: The warrior shouts, increasing the melee attack power of all party members within 20 yards by 232. Lasts 2 min.
  - Note: Rank 4 read 54 attack power for 3 min on the level 38 tauren, the Classic client gives 91 attack power for 2 min at that level. Other ranks not seen.
- **Demoralizing Shout** (fury, Level 34, 10 Rage, Instant)
  - Reduces the melee attack power of all enemies within 10 yards by 103 for 45 sec.
  - Classic: Reduces the melee attack power of all enemies within 10 yards by 146 for 30 sec.
  - Note: Rank 3 read 103 attack power for 45 sec on the level 38 tauren, the Classic client gives 74 for 30 sec at that level. Other ranks not seen.
- **Slam** (fury, Level 30, 15 Rage, Melee Range, 1.5 sec cast, 15 sec cooldown)
  - Slams the opponent, causing weapon damage plus 43.
  - Classic: Slams the opponent, causing weapon damage plus 87.
  - Note: Rank 3 is already known at level 38 (Classic teaches it at 46) and Slam now has a 15 sec cooldown. The rank 3 tooltip read weapon damage plus 43 with a 1.5 sec cast.
- **Defensive Stance** (protection, Level 10, Instant, 1 sec cooldown)
  - A defensive combat stance. Decreases damage taken by 10% and damage caused by 10%. Increases all threat generated by 30%.
  - Classic: A defensive combat stance. Decreases damage taken by 10% and damage caused by 10%. Increases threat generated.
  - Note: The tooltip now states the 30% threat bonus, damage taken and dealt are still 10% each way.
- **Taunt** (protection, Level 10, Melee Range, Instant, 8 sec cooldown)
  - Taunts the target to attack you, but has no effect if the target is already attacking you.
  - Note: Cooldown is 8 sec instead of 10, the effect text is unchanged.
- **Revenge** (protection, Level 34, 5 Rage, Melee Range, Instant, 5 sec cooldown)
  - Instantly counterattack an enemy for 43 to 53 damage and a high amount of threat. Revenge must follow a block, dodge or parry.
  - Classic: Instantly counterattack an enemy for 81 to 99 damage and a high amount of threat. Revenge must follow a block, dodge or parry.
  - Note: Rank 3 read 43 to 53 damage on the level 38 tauren, the Classic client gives 25 to 31. Other ranks not seen.
- **Shield Block** (protection, Level 16, 10 Rage, Instant, 5 sec cooldown)
  - Increases chance to block by 75% for 7 sec, but will only block 2 attacks.
  - Classic: Increases chance to block by 75% for 5 sec, but will only block 1 attack.
  - Note: Now blocks 2 attacks over 7 sec (Classic: 1 attack over 5 sec), which is what the old Improved Shield Block talent used to add.
- **Shield Wall** (protection, Level 28, Instant, 15 min cooldown)
  - Reduces the damage taken from all attacks by 60% for 12 sec.
  - Classic: Reduces the damage taken from melee attacks, ranged attacks and spells by 75% for 10 sec.
  - Note: Now 60% damage reduction for 12 sec on a 15 minute cooldown (Classic: 75% for 10 sec every 30 minutes). Requires Defensive Stance and a shield.

### Removed abilities

- **Intercept** (fury, Level 30, 10 Rage, 25 yd range, Instant, 30 sec cooldown)
  - Charge an enemy, causing 65 damage and stunning it for 3 sec.
  - Note: Not in the level 38 demo warrior's Fury tab, which lists every other Classic Fury spell of that level. Charge gained stance flexibility through the Vanguard talent.

Battle Stance, Heroic Strike, Charge, Rend, Hamstring, Overpower, Mocking Blow, Cleave, Intimidating Shout, Execute, Challenging Shout, Berserker Stance, Berserker Rage, Whirlwind, Pummel, Bloodrage, Sunder Armor, Shield Bash, Disarm
